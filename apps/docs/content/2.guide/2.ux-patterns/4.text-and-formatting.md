A screen reads as part of the app only when its words, dates and links behave like everyone else's. Modules ship
their strings in Danish and English, and dates and money follow the visitor's locale. This page gives the
rules for strings and values, and ends with a checklist that sums up all four UX pattern pages.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-users"}
**For:** module authors and app developers who add or review a screen.
::

## Text

- **Translated strings.** Every string goes through `useModuleT('<namespace>')` with an English fallback. The module
  ships `da` and `en` JSON files. See [Module Kit i18n](/modules/platform/module-kit/i18n).
- **Shared strings.** Open, Edit, Delete and "Not set" come from the `patterns.*` keys of
  [UI](/modules/interface/ui), and an app can override them.
- **Button labels.** Buttons and menu items name the object: "Add unit", "Delete application", "Revoke token". Never a
  bare "Add", "Delete" or "Save", unless the button sits in a section that is clearly about one thing.
- **Dates and money.** Format them with `useFormat()`, so they follow the active locale. Do not call `toLocaleString`
  or `Intl` directly.
- **Links.** Build them with `useLocaleLink().localize`, so a visitor on `/da/...` stays in their locale.

In a table, dates come from `useFormat()` and statuses are status badges (`StatusBadge`):

## Checklist for a new page

1. Pick the area: admin, account or public. Register the route, and add one item to the matching
   [menu](/modules/interface/menu). See [Layouts and navigation](/guide/ux-patterns/layouts-and-navigation).
2. Use the heading for the area. App pages use `PageHeading`, and every app page renders its own: account pages
   through `AccountHeader`. Public pages use `UPageHeader`.
3. Build lists from `TableCard` and `UTable`, with a `RowMenu` and a `UEmpty`. Build detail pages from `SectionCard`s,
   with a breadcrumb. See [Pages](/guide/ux-patterns/pages).
4. Send writes through a `use<Entity>Actions()` composable and a `FormSlideover`. Destructive writes confirm. See
   [Forms and actions](/guide/ux-patterns/forms-and-actions).
5. Send strings through `useModuleT`, with `da` and `en` JSON. Buttons name the object.
6. Check the page in light and dark mode, at desktop width and at 375px.

::callout{color="success" icon="i-lucide-check"}
**What you should see:** the new page looks like its neighbours in the same area, in both colour modes, at 375px and
at desktop width.
::
