title: Guides
description: Task-oriented walkthroughs for building on the Frigear modules, from a first app to a mobile app and your own OAuth server.
---

Guides follow one task from start to finish and link to the [module pages](/modules) for the reference. For the first
app, start with the [Quick start](/getting-started/quick-start).

| Guide | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Quick start](/getting-started/quick-start) | A Nuxt 4 workspace with sign-in, account pages and a user admin, copied from the starter. |
| [Use your own OAuth server](/guides/own-oauth-server) | What an issuer must provide for [`@frigear-nu/oauth-client`](/modules/oauth-client), with recipes for the Frigear OAuth server, frigear.nu, Keycloak, Auth0 and Zitadel. |
