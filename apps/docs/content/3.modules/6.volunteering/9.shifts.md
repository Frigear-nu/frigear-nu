title: Shifts
description: Schedules, shift roles, assignment, swaps, QR check-in and who is on shift now, per operation, built on crew and teams.
---

Shifts is when people work an operation. [Staff](/guide/concepts/glossary) build a schedule of shifts for the
operation's teams, publish it and put people on the shifts. Volunteers see their shifts, trade or give them away as far
as the schedule allows, and check in with a QR pass that a lead of their team scans. Everyone who takes part sees who is on shift now and which lead
is on duty. The people come from [Crew](/modules/volunteering/crew) and [Teams](/modules/volunteering/teams): a person
can hold a shift when they hold a [team role](/guide/concepts/glossary) on its team. The operations themselves are in
[Operations](/modules/volunteering/operations).

The package is `@frigear-nu/shifts`.

## What you get

- **Volunteer pages** (on by default, each can be turned off): my shifts at `/shifts` (a list by day and a month
  calendar), one shift at `/shifts/:assignmentId` (the lead on duty, the QR pass and the swap), the open swaps at
  `/shifts/swaps`, the page of a team's leads at `/shifts/lead/:teamId` and their scanner at
  `/shifts/check-in/:teamId`. With
  [Menu](/modules/interface/menu), a "My shifts" entry in the account menu. With [Mobile](/modules/interface/mobile), a
  Shifts tab on my shifts.
- **Admin area** (with [Admin](/modules/interface/admin) and [Widget](/modules/interface/widget)): a **Shifts** tab on
  the operation page of Operations. It lists the schedules and who is working now; a schedule opens with a timeline per
  day and team, a list, its shift roles and the swaps that wait for approval.
- **Server routes**: my shifts, the pass and my swaps; the marketplace and offers; the lead's check-in, attendance and
  team view; who is on shift now; and a staff API for schedules, roles, shifts, assignments and swaps.
- **Swap emails**: on a new offer, an accepted or rejected offer, a completed swap, a swap that waits for approval and
  a trade aimed at somebody's shift. A trade's emails name both shifts.
- **Hooks**: `shifts:schedule:published`, `shifts:shift:created`, `shifts:shift:updated`, `shifts:assignment:changed`,
  `shifts:swap:offered`, `shifts:swap:accepted`, `shifts:swap:rejected`, `shifts:swap:completed` and
  `shifts:attendance:marked`.

## Quick setup

::steps

### Install Shifts

```bash
pnpm add @frigear-nu/shifts @nuxthub/core
```

### Add it to nuxt.config.ts

List Admin and Widget before Shifts, so the tab registers. Operations needs its admin pages for the tab to have a page
to live on.

```ts [nuxt.config.ts]
export default defineNuxtConfig({
  modules: ['@nuxthub/core', '@frigear-nu/admin', '@frigear-nu/widget', '@frigear-nu/shifts'],
  operations: { settings: { pages: { admin: true, adminOperation: true } } },
  shifts: {
    timeZone: 'Europe/Copenhagen',
  },
})
```

### Generate the migration

:::warning
Without a [migration](/guide/concepts/glossary) the shifts tables do not exist, and every request to them fails.
:::

```bash
pnpm db:generate
```

### Decide who manages shifts

:::warning
Staff routes check permissions through [Authorization](/modules/identity/authorization). Without a role that holds
`shifts:manage`, nobody can build a schedule. The `admin` and `coordinator` roles hold it when your app defines them.
:::

### Build a schedule

Open the operation, then its **Shifts** tab. Create a schedule, then **Create shifts**: pick a team, the first and last
day and the time slots. Open a shift to put people on it, then **Publish schedule**.

::

::callout{color="success" icon="i-lucide-check"}
**What you should see:** an approved volunteer opens `/shifts` and sees their shift. From an hour before it starts, the
shift's page shows a QR code. A BA of the team opens `/shifts/check-in/<teamId>`, scans it and the volunteer is
checked in. The operation's Shifts tab shows them under **Working now**, with the BA as the lead on duty.
::

## Options

Options go under the `shifts` key.

| Option | Type | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `settings.pages.shifts` | `string \| boolean` | on | My shifts. `true` is `/shifts`. `false` turns it off. |
| `settings.pages.shift` | `string \| boolean` | on | One of my shifts. `true` is `/shifts/:assignmentId`; a path must contain `:assignmentId`. |
| `settings.pages.swaps` | `string \| boolean` | on | The open swaps. `true` is `/shifts/swaps`. |
| `settings.pages.checkIn` | `string \| boolean` | on | The scanner of a team's leads. `true` is `/shifts/check-in/:teamId`; a path must contain `:teamId`. |
| `settings.pages.lead` | `string \| boolean` | on | The page of a team's leads: shifts, attendance and swaps to approve. `true` is `/shifts/lead/:teamId`; a path must contain `:teamId`. |
| `settings.operationsTab` | `boolean` | `true` | Add the Shifts tab to the `admin.operations` tab area. |
| `timeZone` | `string` | `Europe/Copenhagen` | The time zone shift times are entered and shown in. |
| `checkInOpensMinutes` | `number` | `60` | How many minutes before a shift starts its QR pass shows and check-in opens. |

**`timeZone`** is the operation's local time. Bulk creation turns days and `HH:mm` slots into instants in it, so
`18:00` stays `18:00` across daylight saving changes; the guards count days in it; and every shifts page and form shows
and takes times in it, whatever the viewer's own time zone. Set `appConfig.frigearUi.timeZone` to the same zone, so other
dates of the app match. An unknown zone stops the build.

## Permissions

Shifts registers these permissions with [Authorization](/modules/identity/authorization).

| Name | Flags | Held by default | What it allows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `shifts:read` | implicit | every signed-in user | Read your own shifts, the marketplace, and who is on shift now where you take part. |
| `shifts:swap` | implicit | every signed-in user | Open a swap of your own shifts, offer on others', accept and reject offers on yours. |
| `shifts:lead` | implicit | every signed-in user | Check people in, mark attendance, approve swaps and read the team view, on teams you lead. |
| `shifts:manage` | | `admin` and `coordinator` | Schedules, shift roles, shifts, assignments and every swap. |
| `shifts:pii` | sensitive | `admin` and `coordinator` | See the email address and phone number of people on a shift. |

- **Scope:** a token may use the named scope `shifts` or `shifts:read` itself. Either reads your own shifts and who is
  on shift now. Swapping and leading need their own scope (`shifts:swap`, `shifts:lead`).
- **Policies:** the implicit permissions pass for everyone without a record, so every route authorizes again with the
  record once it is loaded.
  - `shifts:read`: your own assignments (`{ userId }`); a team (`{ teamId, published }`) you lead, or whose published
    shifts you hold an explicit role on; an operation (`{ operationId }`) you take part in (approved crew or an explicit
    team role there, `takesPart` of Audience); and staff.
  - `shifts:swap`: only your own assignments.
  - `shifts:lead`: the leads of the record's team (`isTeamLead` of Teams: `ba` and up on a bar team) and staff.
- **Why `shifts:pii`:** leads and volunteers see names. Emails and phone numbers are personal data, so they sit behind a
  separate grant. It ends in `:pii`, so `shifts:*` and `*` never cover it.

## Server

Everything under `/api` is in the [Routes](#routes) section. This section covers how shifts work.

### Schedules

A schedule belongs to an operation. It starts as a `draft`: staff build it and volunteers see none of it, not even
their assignments. Publishing makes it `published` (once: there is no way back to draft) and fires
`shifts:schedule:published`. A schedule also sets:

- `coverage`, `handover` and `approval`: how its shifts change hands, see
  [Coverage, handover and approval](#coverage-handover-and-approval).
- `minPerShift`: fewer people shows a shift as short. `maxPerShift`: the capacity of a shift that sets none.
- `guards`: `maxShiftsPerDay` and `minRestHours`, see [Guards](#guards-and-capacity).

**Copying** takes the shift roles and shifts of another schedule (`{ scheduleId, offsetDays }`) and its coverage,
handover and approval, never the people on them. The shifts move by whole days at the same local time (`364` keeps the weekday a year on). A schedule of another
operation maps its teams to this operation's teams by name; a team without a match is skipped and named in
`skippedTeamIds`. A role whose minimum team role is not on the target team kind's ladder is copied without one and
named in `changedRoleIds`.

A schedule with people on its shifts (or people who were on them: attended, no-shows) cannot be deleted (`409
schedule_in_use`), nor can such a shift (`409 shift_in_use`). Cancel the assignments first.

### Shift roles

A shift role is a role on one team of the schedule, such as "BA" or "Bartender", with the lowest team role that may hold
it (`teamRole`, a key of the team kind's ladder; empty is anybody on the team). A shift may name a role: new
assignments on it hold that role unless staff pick another. Moving a shift to another team or role is refused while
people are on it.

### Who can hold a shift

A person can hold an assignment on a shift when they hold an **explicit** active role on the shift's team, and that role
is at least the shift role's `teamRole` on the ladder (`roleAtLeast` of Audience). Explicit means a `team_members` row
that grants: approved crew, or a member staff added in Teams (`teamGrantOf` with `source: 'explicit'`). People who hold
the team role only through a mapping rule (a global `barmanager` is a BA) are not assignable in this release, so a
mapping rule never puts somebody on a shift. Anything else is a `409 not_eligible` with `reason: 'grant'` or `'role'`.

One person holds a shift once (`409 already_assigned`). The rule is checked again after the insert, so two requests at
once cannot put a person on a shift twice.

### Guards and capacity

The capacity of a shift is its own `capacity`, else the schedule's `maxPerShift`; `null` is unlimited. Assigning
somebody to a full shift is a `409 shift_full` unless staff send `force: true`. The capacity is counted again after
the insert, so two requests at once cannot overfill a shift: the last places go to the earliest rows (by creation, then
id, the order of the duplicate check) and only a row past the capacity is taken back.

The guards are computed per assignment, from the person's other `assigned` and `attended` shifts:

| Warning | When |
| --- | --- |
| `overlap` | The person works another shift at the same time. Always, without a guard. |
| `min_rest` | Fewer than `minRestHours` between the end of one shift and the start of the other. |
| `max_shifts_per_day` | More than `maxShiftsPerDay` shifts start on one day (in the time zone). |
| `shift_full` | Over the capacity (only with `force`). |
| `below_min` | The shift still has fewer than `minPerShift` people. |

Staff get the guards as `warnings` next to the assignment; they never block. A volunteer swap is refused when either
person could not hold the shift they get, as they would stand after the exchange. The `409` code names the guard, and
`data.userId` the person:

| Code | When |
| --- | --- |
| `guard_overlap` | The person works another shift at the same time. |
| `guard_rest` | Less than `minRestHours` between two of their shifts. |
| `guard_max_per_day` | More than `maxShiftsPerDay` shifts on one day. |
| `guard_ineligible` | No explicit role on the shift's team, or below the shift role's `teamRole`. |
| `guard_capacity` | The shift holds more people than its capacity (staff forced people on, or lowered it). |

A swap moves people between assignments, so the number of people on a shift never changes and `minPerShift` cannot
break. The guards run when an offer is made (a doomed offer is refused), when a targeted trade is opened, and again
when the owner accepts and when a lead approves.

### Coverage, handover and approval

Three settings of a schedule decide how its shifts change hands between volunteers:

- `coverage`: `all` means volunteers must work every shift they are given: nothing a volunteer does may lower how many
  shifts they hold. `flexible` (the default) lets them give shifts away.
- `handover`: `trade` (shift for shift: both change hands together, so each person keeps their count), `release` (give a
  shift away, or take one over) or `both` (the default).
- `approval`: `peer` (the default: the two volunteers' agreement is enough) or `lead` (a lead of the team, or staff,
  approves each swap the owner accepted).

| `coverage` | `handover` | Volunteers can | Valid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `all` | `trade` | Trade shift for shift. | Yes |
| `all` | `release` or `both` | | No: `400 invalid_schedule_settings` |
| `flexible` | `trade` | Trade shift for shift. | Yes |
| `flexible` | `release` | Give a shift away; take one over. | Yes |
| `flexible` | `both` | Either: the owner picks when opening the swap. | Yes |

`approval` combines with every valid row. `coverage` governs swaps and handovers, not leaving the crew: a person who
withdraws from the crew (or leaves the team) still has their future shifts cancelled.

**Across schedules.** A trade may give back a shift of another schedule. Both schedules must allow a trade (`409
handover_not_allowed`), and when either has `coverage: 'all'` both shifts must be in the same schedule (`409
handover_cross_schedule`), so a shift that must be worked cannot be traded into a schedule that lets it be given away.
The swap needs a lead's approval when either schedule says `approval: 'lead'`; a lead of either team, or staff,
approves it. Creating a schedule and changing one (`PATCH`, checked merged with the stored
values) answer `400 invalid_schedule_settings` for the invalid rows.

**Roskilde** asks every volunteer to work all their shifts, and lets them swap one shift for another between
themselves:

```json
{ "coverage": "all", "handover": "trade", "approval": "peer" }
```

The server refuses every path that would lower a volunteer's count unless the schedule allows a release, with `409
handover_not_allowed`: opening a release, an offer without a shift in return, and accepting or approving such an offer.
Opening a trade under `handover: 'release'` is refused the same way. Accepting and approving check the settings of that
moment, so a release opened before staff switched a schedule to `coverage: 'all'` can no longer be carried out (staff or
its owner cancel it), and the marketplace stops showing it. The lead page and the admin tab hide **Approve swap** on such
a swap and say to reject it. Opening a swap again keeps the live one while it is of the kind asked for and still
allowed; a stale `open` one (another kind, or no longer allowed) is cancelled and replaced, and one with offers answers
`409 swap_changed`. The pages hide every action the schedule does not allow and
say "trade only: you must work all your shifts" when `coverage` is `all`.

::note
**Swap model changed.** Schedules used to have a single swap mode (`peer` or `approval`) and any swap could be taken
over without a shift in return. `approval` replaces it (`approval` became `lead`, migrated with the data), and
`coverage` and `handover` are new. Swaps have a `kind`; swaps opened before the change are releases. Hook payloads carry
`kind`, the swap and schedule DTOs carry the settings, and the guard refusals answer `guard_*` codes instead of
`swap_guard` and `not_eligible`. Apps that open swaps through the API pass `{ kind }` where the schedule allows both.
::

### Swaps

The owner of an `assigned` shift in a published schedule that has not started opens a swap of it, of a kind the
schedule allows (`{ kind }`; left out, a release where the schedule allows one, else a trade):

- **A trade** asks for a shift in return. Every offer gives one of the offerer's own future shifts
  (`{ assignmentId }`, required), and both shifts change hands together, so each person's count stays the same. The
  owner may aim the trade at one assignment (`wantedAssignmentId`): only its holder sees the swap and may offer, and only
  that shift (`409 not_wanted`); the holder is emailed. The owner chooses among the offers; each names its shift.
- **A release** gives the shift away. An offer takes it over and gives nothing back (`400` with a shift).

Both people must be able to hold the shift they get ([eligibility](#who-can-hold-a-shift) and guards): a swap across teams works only when each person holds a role on
the other's team. One live offer per person and swap (`409 offer_exists`; two offers at once keep the earliest). A shift
whose own swap is being carried out or waits for approval cannot be given back (`409 not_swappable`).

```text
open ──offer──► offered ──owner accepts──► accepted ──► completed          (approval: peer)
                   │                └────► pending_approval ──approve──► accepted ──► completed
                   │                                 └──reject──► open      (approval: lead)
                   └──last offer withdrawn or rejected──► open
open, offered, pending_approval ──owner withdraws──► cancelled
```

- **Accepting** checks the schedule's settings and both directions again, then claims the swap with a conditional write, so two accepts at once
  carry out one; the other gets `409 swap_changed`. The other waiting offers become `superseded`.
- **Completing** hands the owner's assignment to the offerer and, in a trade, the shift given back to the owner.
  Each assignment keeps its id and gets a new check-in code. Every write is conditional: the accepted offer must still
  stand (a withdraw that won the race leaves none), the owner's assignment must still be `assigned` to the person who
  opened the swap and the one given back to the offerer, and the swap must still be `accepted`. On postgres the writes
  run in one transaction. On sqlite and D1, which have no interactive transactions, a failed write puts the earlier ones
  back, each undo conditional on the row still holding what the exchange wrote. When the exchange does not happen the
  offer is rejected and the swap is open again (`409 swap_changed`). Other live swaps of the shift given back are
  cancelled, and offers elsewhere that gave either shift are withdrawn.
- **Recovery:** on sqlite and D1 a process that dies between the writes leaves the swap `accepted` with one shift moved.
  Staff settle it with `POST /api/admin/shifts/swaps/:swapId/cancel` (completed when both shifts moved, put back to the
  people who held them when only one moved, else cancelled), and the owner's withdraw does the same once the swap has
  sat in `accepted` for five minutes. Between the crash and the recovery the two people may both, or neither, hold a
  shift: that window is what remains without transactions.
- **`approval: 'peer'`** fires `shifts:swap:accepted` only once the exchange is done, just before
  `shifts:swap:completed`, and sends only the completed email. `approval: 'lead'` fires `accepted` and sends the
  accepted email when the owner accepts.
- **`approval: 'lead'`:** the leads of the swap's team and of the team of the shift given back are emailed; any of
  them, or staff, approves (the settings and the exchange are checked again and the swap completes) or rejects (the
  offer is rejected and the swap is open for offers again).
- **Withdrawing:** the owner cancels an open swap; an offerer withdraws a waiting offer, or an accepted one that still
  waits for approval (the swap is open again). A withdraw and an approval at once settle one way: when the approval
  claimed the swap first the offer is put back and the withdraw answers `409 swap_changed`.
- **Cancelled shifts:** when an assignment stops being `assigned` (cancelled, checked in, marked a no-show), its swap is
  cancelled and offers that gave it back are withdrawn.
- The `rejected` swap status is reserved: an approver's rejection puts the swap back to `open` instead.

### Check-in and attendance

Every assignment has a check-in code: 64 hex characters, like an Events ticket. The pass route shows it, and its QR code
as SVG, only from `checkInOpensMinutes` before the shift starts until it ends, and only to the person. A lead of the team
(or staff) checks the person in with `POST /api/shifts/teams/:teamId/check-in { code }`:

- an unknown code, or the code of another team's shift, is a `404` (a lead learns nothing of other teams);
- a cancelled assignment is `409 assignment_cancelled`; outside the window `409 outside_window`;
- a person who no longer holds an explicit role on the team (removed, or a membership status that stopped granting) is
  `409 not_eligible`, and their future shifts on the team are cancelled there and then. The pass route does the same:
  no code for them;
- scanning twice is fine: the second scan answers `alreadyCheckedIn: true` with the first scan's time.

A check-in marks the assignment `attended`. Leads and staff also mark attendance by hand (`attended`, `no_show`),
for example after the window closed, but not before check-in opens (`409 outside_window`). An attended assignment
cannot be cancelled.

### On shift now

`GET /api/shifts/operations/:operationId/now` answers the shifts of the operation's published schedules running now,
per team, with the people on them (`assigned` and `attended`) and the leads among them: the BA on duty. People who take
part in the operation and staff read it. Names always; email and phone number only with `shifts:pii`.

### People leaving a team

Shifts listens to the Teams hooks of teams an operation owns:

- `teams:member:removed` (removed in Teams, or a crew member rejected, waitlisted, withdrawn or moved to another team):
  the person's future `assigned` assignments on the team are cancelled.
- `teams:member:role-changed`: the future ones whose shift role the new role no longer meets are cancelled.

Each cancellation fires `shifts:assignment:changed` and cancels the assignment's swap.

### Emails

| Template | To | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `shifts-swap-proposed` | The holder of the wanted shift | A trade is aimed at their shift. |
| `shifts-swap-offered` | The owner | Somebody offered on their swap. |
| `shifts-swap-accepted` | The offerer | The owner accepted their offer. |
| `shifts-swap-rejected` | The offerer (and the owner on an approver's rejection) | The offer was turned down. |
| `shifts-swap-completed` | Both people | The shift (both shifts, in a trade) changed hands. |
| `shifts-swap-pending` | The explicit leads of the team(s) | A swap waits for approval. |

A trade's emails name both shifts (the template gets `trade: true`). The emails go through
[Notify](/modules/platform/notify) in the locale on the person's crew row of the team (`en`
without one). Their link goes to the shift's page for the owner, the lead page for a pending approval, my shifts once
completed and the open swaps otherwise. Links need a configured site origin (the one Auth uses for its emails); without
one the emails go out without a link and a warning is logged once. A failing email is logged and never fails the swap.

## App

### Pages

| Page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `/shifts` | My shifts in published schedules, grouped by day, with a month calendar marking the days I work; past shifts on request. |
| `/shifts/:assignmentId` | One of my shifts: when and where, my role, the lead on duty, the QR pass while check-in is open, and the swap with its offers: **Offer shift for a trade** and **Give shift away**, as far as the schedule allows. |
| `/shifts/swaps` | The swaps I can take: a trade asks me to choose one of my own shifts, a released shift is taken over; my own shifts on offer. |

| `/shifts/lead/:teamId` | The page of a team's leads: its upcoming shifts with attendance marks, the swaps waiting for approval, and the scanner. My shifts links it for the teams the person leads. |

Signed-out visitors go to the sign-in page and come back.

### Tab on the operation page

The **Shifts** tab (`admin.operations`, order 30, after Crew and Teams, `shifts:manage`) lists the operation's schedules
and who is working now. `?schedule=<id>` opens one schedule:

- **Day:** a timeline per day, a row per team, a block per shift coloured by how full it is (short of the minimum,
  full). Clicking a block opens the shift: who is on it, marking attendance, taking people off, assigning people from a
  search of the team with the guards each would break (for the shift role picked in the form), and editing its times,
  capacity and role.
- **List:** every shift with its people.
- **Shift roles:** add, edit and delete them.
- **Swaps to approve:** the swaps of the schedule waiting for approval, with approve and reject.
- **Settings:** coverage, handover and approval as form rows (also in the schedule's slideover). Picking
  `coverage: 'all'` sets `handover: 'trade'`.

The schedule's header holds **Edit schedule**, **Copy shifts**, **Create shifts** (a range of days and time slots for one
team) and **Publish schedule**. The tab reads the operation's teams through a shifts route, so `shifts:manage` is enough.

## Hooks

Your app answers these [Nitro](/guide/concepts/glossary) hooks from a server plugin (`server/plugins/*.ts`).

| Hook | When | Payload |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `shifts:schedule:published` | After a draft schedule was published. | `{ event, schedule }` |
| `shifts:shift:created` | After a shift was created (bulk creation and copying fire it per shift). | `{ event, shift }` |
| `shifts:shift:updated` | After a shift's times, place, capacity, notes, team or role changed. | `{ event, shift, previous }` |
| `shifts:assignment:changed` | After an assignment was created, cancelled, marked or changed hands in a swap. | `{ event, assignment, previous }` |
| `shifts:swap:offered` | After somebody offered on a swap. | `{ event, kind, swap, offer }` |
| `shifts:swap:accepted` | After the owner accepted an offer. | `{ event, kind, swap, offer }` |
| `shifts:swap:rejected` | After the owner turned an offer down, or an approver rejected the swap. | `{ event, kind, swap, offer }` |
| `shifts:swap:completed` | After the shift (both shifts, in a trade) changed hands. | `{ event, kind, swap, offer }` |
| `shifts:attendance:marked` | After a check-in, or a lead marked attended or a no-show. | `{ event, assignment, by }` |

- `previous` of `shifts:assignment:changed` is `null` for a new assignment. A swap moves an assignment to another
  person: `previous.userId` is the person who gave it away. It fires for assignments in draft schedules too; a listener
  that only cares about what volunteers see checks that the schedule is published.
- `assignment` never carries the check-in code.
- `by` is the user id of the lead or staff member.
- `kind` is the swap's: `trade` or `release`.
- With `approval: 'peer'` an accept fires `shifts:swap:accepted` only after the exchange succeeded, then `shifts:swap:completed`,
  in the same request.
- Shifts listens to `teams:member:removed` and `teams:member:role-changed`.

## Routes

Typed path builders are exported as `shiftsApi` from `@frigear-nu/shifts/api`.

| Route | Who | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/shifts/me` | `shifts:read` | My shifts in published schedules (`?from&to`, ISO instants). |
| `GET /api/shifts/me/leads` | `shifts:read` | The teams I lead, with their operations. |
| `GET /api/shifts/me/:assignmentId` | `shifts:read` + policy | One of my shifts, with the leads on duty and my swap. `404` for somebody else's or a draft's. |
| `GET /api/shifts/me/:assignmentId/pass` | `shifts:read` + policy | The QR pass: `code` and `svg` only inside the window, and only while I hold my role on the team. |
| `POST /api/shifts/me/:assignmentId/swap` | `shifts:swap` + policy | Open a swap `{ kind?, wantedAssignmentId? }` (`201`). Again answers the live swap of that kind; a stale `open` one is replaced. `409 not_swappable`, `handover_not_allowed`, `swap_changed`, and for a targeted trade `handover_cross_schedule` and `guard_*`. |
| `DELETE /api/shifts/me/:assignmentId/swap` | `shifts:swap` + policy | Withdraw the swap (`204`); a swap stuck in `accepted` for five minutes is settled. |
| `GET /api/shifts/swaps` | `shifts:read` | The swaps I can take (of a kind the schedule allows now), each with `myOffer`. |
| `POST /api/shifts/swaps/:swapId/offers` | `shifts:swap` | Offer `{ assignmentId }` on a trade, `{}` on a release (`201`). `400` a shift on a release; `409 handover_not_allowed`, `handover_cross_schedule`, `not_wanted`, `guard_*`, `already_assigned`, `offer_exists`, `own_swap`, `swap_closed`. |

| `POST /api/shifts/swaps/:swapId/offers/:offerId/accept` | `shifts:swap` + policy (owner) | Accept: `completed` (`approval: 'peer'`) or `pending_approval`. `409 handover_not_allowed`, `guard_*`, `swap_changed`. |
| `POST /api/shifts/swaps/:swapId/offers/:offerId/reject` | `shifts:swap` + policy (owner) | Turn the offer down. |
| `POST /api/shifts/swaps/:swapId/approve` | `shifts:lead` + policy | Approve a swap waiting for approval. `409 handover_not_allowed`, `guard_*`, `swap_changed`. |
| `POST /api/shifts/swaps/:swapId/reject` | `shifts:lead` + policy | Reject it: the swap is open again. |
| `POST /api/shifts/teams/:teamId/check-in` | `shifts:lead` + policy | Check in `{ code }`: `{ ok, alreadyCheckedIn, checkedInAt, assignment, shift }`. `404`, `409 outside_window`, `assignment_cancelled`. |
| `GET /api/shifts/teams/:teamId/shifts` | `shifts:lead` + policy | The team's published shifts with their people (`?from&to`). |
| `GET /api/shifts/teams/:teamId/swaps` | `shifts:lead` + policy | The team's swaps (`?status`, e.g. `pending_approval`), also those giving one of its shifts back. |
| `POST /api/shifts/assignments/:assignmentId/attended` | `shifts:lead` + policy | Mark attended. `409 outside_window` before check-in opens. |
| `POST /api/shifts/assignments/:assignmentId/no-show` | `shifts:lead` + policy | Mark a no-show. `409 outside_window` before check-in opens. |
| `GET /api/shifts/operations/:operationId/now` | `shifts:read` + policy | Who is on shift now, per team, with the leads on duty. |
| `GET /api/admin/shifts/schedules` | `shifts:manage` | Schedules (`?operationId`) with their shift counts. |
| `POST /api/admin/shifts/schedules` | `shifts:manage` | Create `{ operationId, name, coverage?, handover?, approval?, minPerShift?, maxPerShift?, guards? }` (`201`, a draft). `400 invalid_schedule_settings`. |
| `GET /api/admin/shifts/schedules/:scheduleId` | `shifts:manage` | One schedule. |
| `PATCH /api/admin/shifts/schedules/:scheduleId` | `shifts:manage` | Change the name, coverage, handover, approval, sizes or guards. `400 invalid_schedule_settings` (checked merged with the stored settings). |

| `POST /api/admin/shifts/schedules/:scheduleId/publish` | `shifts:manage` | Publish. Again is a no-op. |
| `POST /api/admin/shifts/schedules/:scheduleId/copy-from` | `shifts:manage` | Copy `{ scheduleId, offsetDays }`: `{ schedule, roles, shifts, skippedTeamIds, changedRoleIds }`. |
| `GET /api/admin/shifts/schedules/:scheduleId/roles` | `shifts:manage` | The shift roles. |
| `POST /api/admin/shifts/schedules/:scheduleId/roles` | `shifts:manage` | Add `{ teamId, teamRole?, name }` (`201`). |
| `PATCH /api/admin/shifts/roles/:roleId` | `shifts:manage` | Change `{ teamRole?, name? }`. |
| `DELETE /api/admin/shifts/roles/:roleId` | `shifts:manage` | Delete (`204`). `409 role_in_use`. |
| `GET /api/admin/shifts/schedules/:scheduleId/shifts` | `shifts:manage` | The shifts with their people (`?teamId&from&to`). |
| `POST /api/admin/shifts/schedules/:scheduleId/shifts/bulk` | `shifts:manage` | Create `{ teamId, days, slots, capacity?, shiftRoleId?, location?, notes? }` (`201`, at most 500). |

| `POST /api/admin/shifts/:shiftId/assignments` | `shifts:manage` | Assign `{ userId, shiftRoleId?, force? }`: `{ assignment, warnings }` (`201`). `409 not_eligible`, `already_assigned`, `shift_full`. |
| `GET /api/admin/shifts/:shiftId/candidates` | `shifts:manage` | The team's people staff could assign (`?q`, `?shiftRoleId`, the shift's own role by default), with `eligible`, `reason` and `warnings`. |
| `DELETE /api/admin/shifts/assignments/:assignmentId` | `shifts:manage` | Cancel (`204`). `409 invalid_transition` for an attended one. |
| `GET /api/admin/shifts/swaps` | `shifts:manage` | Swaps with their offers (`?operationId&status`). |
| `POST /api/admin/shifts/swaps/:swapId/cancel` | `shifts:manage` | Cancel a live swap; settle an `accepted` one stuck halfway. |
| `GET /api/admin/shifts/operations/:operationId/teams` | `shifts:manage` | The operation's teams with their kind's ladder, for the schedule forms. |

Emails and phone numbers need `shifts:pii`; without it a person has neither field. Reads that leads and volunteers make
live under `/api/shifts`; `/api/admin/shifts` is for staff.

## Database

Tables the module adds. Generate and commit a [migration](/get-started/database#migrations) after installing.

| Table | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `shift_schedules` | `shs_` id, `operationId`, `name` (json), `status` (`draft`, `published`), `coverage` (`all`, `flexible`), `handover` (`trade`, `release`, `both`), `approval` (`peer`, `lead`), `minPerShift`, `maxPerShift`, `guards` (json), `publishedAt`. |
| `shift_roles` | `shr_` id, `scheduleId`, `teamId`, `teamRole` (the lowest team role, or `null`), `name` (json). |
| `shifts` | `shf_` id, `scheduleId`, `teamId`, `shiftRoleId`, `startsAt`, `endsAt`, `location` (json: `name`, `mapId`, `featureId`), `capacity`, `notes` (json). |
| `shift_assignments` | `sha_` id, `shiftId`, `userId`, `shiftRoleId`, `status` (`assigned`, `attended`, `no_show`, `cancelled`), `checkInCode` (unique), `checkedInAt`, `checkedInBy`, `assignedBy`. |
| `shift_swaps` | `shw_` id, `assignmentId`, `userId` (who opened it), `kind` (`trade`, `release`), `wantedAssignmentId` (a targeted trade), `status`, `openedAt`, `decidedAt`. |
| `shift_swap_offers` | `sho_` id, `swapId`, `userId`, `offeredAssignmentId` (the shift given in a trade; `null` takes a released shift over), `status` (`offered`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `withdrawn`, `superseded`), `decidedAt`, `approvedBy`. |

Operation, team and user ids carry no foreign key: those tables belong to other modules. Every table has a
`.postgresql.ts` twin, and the schema parity test covers them. Runtime code uses no transactions, except the swap
exchange on postgres: the conditional writes above keep the rows consistent on both dialects.

## Testing notes

The e2e fixture runs on sqlite with Operations, Teams, Forms, Notify and Crew, without Authorization or Email
Templates. Roles come from a role provider by email, and the emails are captured by a `notify:email` listener over
notify's log driver. People get their team roles through the real Teams routes (members added by hand), which is the
same explicit grant an approved crew member holds; crew's own approval flow is tested in Crew. Check-in tests move a
shift's times around now with `PATCH`. The postgresql branch has no end-to-end test; the schema twins are checked by
the parity test.

## Not in this release

- **Dashboard items** (next shift, open swaps, team on shift now), through `extendDashboard` once the dashboard module
  lands.
- **Reminders, the iCal feed, broadcasts and perks.** They come in the next phase, with their own tables.
- **The maps layer** (`shifts.on-duty`): a shift's `location` already holds `mapId` and `featureId` for it.
- **Mapped-only people on shifts.** Somebody whose team role comes only from a mapping rule cannot be assigned; whether
  Teams should turn such a person into a membership is an open question of Teams.
- **Website wiring.**

## Open questions

- Should leads assign people on their own team, not only staff?
- Should a membership status that stops granting (not only a removal) fire `teams:member:removed`, so future shifts are
  cancelled at once rather than when the pass or check-in finds it?
- Should a published schedule be able to go back to draft?
- Should a swap offer expire when the shift is close?
