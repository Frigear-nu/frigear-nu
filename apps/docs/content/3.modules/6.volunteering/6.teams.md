---
title: Teams
description: Teams owned by an operation or the platform, members with a role from their team kind's ladder, and mapping rules that give people a team role without adding them by hand.
---

Teams holds the teams crew work in: the bars of a festival operation, a first aid team of the platform. Each team
has a kind, and the kind's ladder holds the roles its members can have: a bar team's ladder is volunteer, bartender,
BA and coordinator. [Staff](/guide/concepts/glossary) add members by hand, or add mapping rules so that everyone with
a global role or a plan holds a team role on their own: a global `barmanager` is a BA in every bar of an operation.
It is the source of team roles for the other [volunteering](/modules/volunteering) modules. For permissions that
code checks, use [Authorization](/modules/identity/authorization) instead.

The package is `@frigear-nu/teams`. It answers the `team` [owner](/modules/volunteering#owners) and the viewer hook of
[Audience](/modules/volunteering/audience).

## What you get

- **Admin area** (opt-in, with [Admin](/modules/interface/admin)): a team list at `/admin/teams`, filterable by owner,
  and a team page at `/admin/teams/:id` with its members, mapping rules and the ladder of its kind. The sidebar entry
  needs [Menu](/modules/interface/menu). With [Widget](/modules/interface/widget), a Teams tab on the operation page of
  Operations (`@frigear-nu/operations`).
- **Server routes** (on by default): your own teams and roles, a team's page and members, and a staff API for teams,
  members, mapping rules and ladders.
- **Server helpers** (auto-imported): `resolveTeamRoles`, `teamMembers` and `isTeamLead` for other modules.
- **Hooks**: `teams:member:added`, `teams:member:removed`, `teams:member:role-changed`, and the provider hooks
  `teams:roles:resolve` and `teams:members:candidates`.
- **OAuth claim**: a `teams` claim in id tokens and userinfo, with [OAuth Server](/modules/identity/oauth-server).
- **Emails.** None built in. Send one from `teams:member:added`.

## Quick setup

::steps

### Install Teams

```bash
pnpm add @frigear-nu/teams @nuxthub/core
```

### Add it to nuxt.config.ts

List Admin (or Widget and Menu) before Teams, so the tab and the menu entry register.

```ts [nuxt.config.ts]
export default defineNuxtConfig({
  modules: ['@nuxthub/core', '@frigear-nu/admin', '@frigear-nu/teams'],
  teams: {
    settings: {
      pages: { admin: true, adminTeam: true },
    },
  },
})
```

### Generate the migration

:::warning
Without a [migration](/guide/concepts/glossary) the teams tables do not exist, and every request to them fails.
:::

```bash
pnpm db:generate
```

### Decide who manages teams

:::warning
Staff routes check permissions through [Authorization](/modules/identity/authorization). Without a role that holds
`teams:manage`, nobody can manage teams. The `admin` and `coordinator` roles hold it when your app defines them.
:::

::

::callout{color="success" icon="i-lucide-check"}
**What you should see:** a coordinator opens an operation's Teams tab and adds "Main bar". They add a mapping rule
"global role `barmanager` is `ba`" for every team of the operation. A user with the global role `barmanager` now sees
the bar with the role `ba` and `source: 'mapped'` at `GET /api/teams/me`, without being added.
::

## Options

Options go under the `teams` key.

| Option | Type | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ladders` | `Record<kind, { roles, leadFrom? }>` | the `bar` ladder | The built-in ladders by team kind, stored on the first request. A kind here replaces the default one. |
| `settings.pages.admin` | `string \| boolean` | off | The staff team list. `true` is `/admin/teams`. |
| `settings.pages.adminTeam` | `string \| boolean` | off | The staff team page. `true` is `/admin/teams/:id`. |
| `settings.operationsTab` | `boolean` | `true` | Add the Teams tab to the `admin.operations` tab area. |

**`ladders`** roles are `{ key, label }`, lowest first. Keys are lower case (`a-z`, `0-9`, `-`, `_`). `leadFrom` is
the lowest role that leads a team of that kind. An invalid ladder fails the build. A kind is stored on the first boot
that sees it; changing its option later does not overwrite the stored row, so edit a stored ladder in admin.

```ts [nuxt.config.ts]
export default defineNuxtConfig({
  teams: {
    ladders: {
      firstaid: {
        roles: [{ key: 'helper', label: { en: 'Helper', da: 'Hjælper' } }, { key: 'medic', label: 'Medic' }],
        leadFrom: 'medic',
      },
    },
  },
})
```

## Permissions

Teams registers these permissions with [Authorization](/modules/identity/authorization).

| Name | Flags | Held by default | What it allows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `teams:read` | implicit | every signed-in user | Read the teams you belong to, your role and who else is on them. |
| `teams:lead` | implicit | every signed-in user | Lead the teams where you hold a lead role. Other modules check it on a team. |
| `teams:manage` | | `admin` and `coordinator` | Create teams, manage members, mapping rules and ladders. |
| `teams:pii` | sensitive | `admin` and `coordinator` | See the email addresses of team members. |

- **Scope:** a token may use the named scope `teams` or `teams:read` itself. Either reads your teams and their
  members, never the staff routes. `teams:pii` is usable as a scope too.
- **Why `teams:pii`:** a member list shows names and avatars to everyone on the team. Emails are other people's
  contact details, so they sit behind a separate grant. It ends in `:pii`, so it is sensitive: `teams:*` and `*`
  never cover it, and it is only held when granted by name.
- **Policy on `teams:read`:** a team is readable by the people who hold a role on it, explicit or mapped, and by
  callers with `teams:manage`. Anyone else gets `403`.
- **Policy on `teams:lead`:** the caller holds a role on the record's team at or above the ladder's `leadFrom` (`ba`
  and up on a bar team), explicit or mapped. The record is a team, or any record with a `teamId`.

```ts
// In another module's policy, for a record with a teamId:
definePolicy('shifts:edit', async ({ event, user, record }) => record ? isTeamLead(event, user.id, record.teamId) : undefined)
```

## Server

Everything under `/api` is in the [Routes](#routes) section. This section covers what other modules build on.

### Team roles

```ts
resolveTeamRoles(event, userId, { type: 'operation', id: operationId }) // TeamRoleGrant[] on that owner
```

This is the [team-role contract](/modules/volunteering#team-role-contract). A grant is
`{ ownerType, ownerId, teamId, kind, role, source }`, and `source` is `explicit` or `mapped`.

1. **Explicit memberships** are the rows of `team_members` whose `status` is `null` or `'active'`. They come first.
   See [Membership status](#membership-status).
2. **Mapping rules** give the other teams a mapped grant. A `role` rule matches a global role of the user, closed over
   `inherit`, from Auth's `getEffectiveRoles`. A `plan` rule matches an active subscription. Listeners of
   `teams:roles:resolve` add more.
3. **Explicit beats mapped** on the same team, whatever the rule.
4. **Between mapped rules** on one team, the highest `priority` wins, then the higher role on the ladder.
5. A rule whose `teamRole` is not on a team's ladder skips that team.

Mapped grants are never stored. Every request reads the rules and the user's roles again, and caches the result for
the rest of that request. `teams:roles:resolve` fires only when there are rules in play: a source with no rule rows
gives no grants.

**Errors fail closed.** A `teams:roles:resolve` listener that throws loses the grants it pushed (and so do the
listeners after it); the explicit memberships and teams' own role rules still count. A plan that cannot be checked is
not held. The viewer hook leaves the viewer without team roles, and the OAuth claim is left out, when resolution
fails. Each failure is logged.

### Membership status

`status` on a membership belongs to the module that uses it: crew sets it next to its own crew status. Teams reads
one thing from it: a row grants its role only when `status` is `null` or `'active'`. Any other value, such as
`'pending'` or `'inactive'`, keeps the person listed on the team (for staff and leads, with the status) without a
grant: they cannot read the team, do not lead it, and are not in the viewer. A mapping rule may still give them a
mapped role. The members list shows `status` only to callers with `teams:manage` or a lead role on the team. `isTeamLead(event, userId, teamId)` and `teamMembers(event, teamId, { mapped })` use the same
cache.

### Mapping rules

A rule belongs to an owner, like a team: `ownerType` and `ownerId`, with `ownerId: null` for the platform.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `teamId` | One team of the owner, or `null` for every team of the owner. |
| `match` | `{ role: 'barmanager' }` or `{ plan: 'yearly' }`. Exactly one key; anything else is `400`. |
| `teamRole` | A role key of the team's ladder. For every team, a key of at least one ladder. |
| `priority` | An integer, `0` by default. Higher wins. |

`role` rules work out of the box. `plan` rules need [Subscriptions](/modules/commerce/subscriptions): Teams answers
them from a server plugin it registers only then, with `hasActiveSubscription`. Plans are identified by their plan key.

### Ladders

Each team kind has one ladder in `team_role_ladders`. The module option's ladders, the `bar` one by default, are
stored on the first request when their kind has no row yet. They are never overwritten, so admins may edit them.
Until a kind is stored, the option's ladder applies.

A ladder cannot drop a role that an explicit member of a team of that kind holds, that a rule for one such team maps
to, or that a rule for every team of an owner maps to when that owner has a team of the kind: `409` with the code
`team_role_in_use` and the roles in `data.roles`.

### Members

`teamMembers(event, teamId, { mapped?, pii? })` lists the explicit members, highest role first. With `mapped: true` it
also lists the people a rule gives a role on the team. Mapped members are not rows, so Teams finds the candidates and
checks each one:

- users assigned, in [Authorization](/modules/identity/authorization), a role the team's rules name or a role that
  inherits one (read from Auth's compiled role table), when Authorization is installed;
- users with an entitled subscription (`active` or `trialing`, not ended) to a plan the rules name, when
  Subscriptions is installed;
- the user ids that `teams:members:candidates` listeners add.

A role that comes only from `defaultRoles`, an `auto` role or an `authorization:roles` listener cannot be enumerated,
so those people are not listed, although they do hold the role. At most 1000 candidates are checked. That is why
mapped members are on demand only: `?include=mapped` on the API (staff with `teams:manage` and the team's leads,
`403` for anyone else), and a switch on the team page that starts off. A candidate source that fails is logged and
adds nobody.

### Audience answers

Teams answers both Audience hooks from a server plugin it always registers:

- **`audience:viewer:resolve`**: every grant of the user on every owner goes into `viewer.teamRoles`, and the ladder of
  each kind among them into `viewer.ladders`. It identifies the user from `ctx.user`, and uses `viewer.globalRoles`
  when the caller filled them in.
- **`audience:owner:resolve`** for `owner.type === 'team'`: `label` is the team's name, `exists` whether the id is found.

### Owners

A team is created for an owner. The platform has no id (`ownerType: 'platform'`, `ownerId: null`). Any other owner
must exist according to `audience:owner:resolve`: Operations answers `operation`. An unknown id, or an owner type no
installed module answers, is `400`. The owner and the kind of a team are fixed once it exists.

### Maps

With [Maps](/modules/volunteering/maps) installed, a server plugin answers `maps:edit:resolve` for maps whose owner
type is `team`: the team's leads (`isTeamLead`, a role at or above the ladder's `leadFrom`, explicit or mapped) may draw
on the team's map. The user comes from `ctx.user`, so it works for tokens too. Staff keep editing through
`maps:manage`, and a lead cannot edit another team's map or an operation's. Teams does not import Maps, and without it
the plugin is not in the build.

### OAuth claim

With [OAuth Server](/modules/identity/oauth-server) installed, an application granted `teams` or `teams:read` gets a
`teams` claim in the id token and from userinfo:

```json
{ "teams": [{ "id": "tem_...", "ownerType": "operation", "ownerId": "opr_...", "kind": "bar", "role": "ba", "source": "mapped" }] }
```

Access tokens never carry it, and an existing `teams` claim is never replaced. The same application reads the members
of those teams with `GET /api/teams/:id/members` and a bearer token.

## App

### Pages

| Page | Path | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team list | `/admin/teams` | Staff with `teams:manage`. Search, an owner filter (`?ownerType=&ownerId=`) and sortable columns. |
| Team page | `/admin/teams/:id` | Staff with `teams:manage`. Key facts, members, mapping rules and the kind's ladder. |

The member table's row menu offers Change role and Remove member. For a mapped member it offers Assign role, which
adds them explicitly. Add member opens a slideover that searches people by name or email. Rules and the ladder are
edited in slideovers too.

### Tab on the operation page

Teams adds a **Teams** tab to the `admin.operations` tab area of [Widget](/modules/interface/widget), with the prop
`ownerType: 'operation'`. It reads the operation's `id` from the tab context and never imports Operations. It lists
the operation's teams and adds new ones for it. Rendered without an `operation`, it shows an empty state and queries
nothing. Turn it off with `settings.operationsTab: false`.

### Composables

| Composable | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `useTeamActions()` | `create`, `edit`, `remove` and `menu(team)`, with toasts and confirmations. |
| `useTeamMemberActions(team, ladders)` | `add`, `changeRole`, `remove` and `menu(member)`. |
| `useTeamMappingActions(context)` | `create`, `edit`, `remove` and `menu(rule)`. |
| `useTeamLinks()` | Locale-aware links to the admin pages. |

## Hooks

Your app answers these [Nitro](/guide/concepts/glossary) hooks from a server plugin (`server/plugins/*.ts`).

| Hook | When | Payload |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `teams:member:added` | When an explicit membership starts granting: added with `status` null or `active`, or its status became one of those. | `{ event, team, member }` |
| `teams:member:removed` | When an explicit membership stops granting: removed while it granted, or its status left `null` / `active`. | `{ event, team, member }` |
| `teams:member:role-changed` | When the role of a membership that grants changed. | `{ event, team, member, from, to }` |
| `teams:roles:resolve` | While resolving a user's mapped grants. Push grants into `grants`. | `{ event, user, owner, globalRoles, rules, teams, ladders, grants }` |
| `teams:members:candidates` | While listing mapped members. Add user ids to `userIds`. | `{ event, team, rules, userIds }` |

- `team` is the staff DTO and `member` the member DTO with the email, whatever the caller may see.
- **`teams:roles:resolve`.** `owner` is `null`: one resolution covers every owner. `event` is `undefined` while the
  OAuth claim is built. `rules` are the rules in play, `teams` the teams they cover. Give a pushed grant the `priority`
  of the rule it stands for; `mappedGrantsFor(teams, rules, ladders)` from `@frigear-nu/teams/utils` builds them.
- **The member hooks follow the grant, not the row.** A pending row fires nothing when it is added, deleted or given
  another role; `added` fires when its status becomes `active` (with the role it has then), and `removed` when an
  active membership becomes `inactive`. Crew fires its own hooks on the same transitions, so both agree on when a
  person starts and stops working on a team. Mapped grants fire no hooks.
- Deleting a team fires no `teams:member:removed` for the memberships that go with it.

## Routes

Typed path builders are exported as `teamsApi` from `@frigear-nu/teams/api`.

| Route | Who | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/teams/me` | `teams:read` | Your grants on every owner, explicit first, each with `source` and the team's `name`. |
| `GET /api/teams/:id` | `teams:read` + policy | One team. |
| `GET /api/teams/:id/members` | `teams:read` + policy | Names and avatars; `email` only with `teams:pii`; `status` for staff and leads. `?include=mapped` (staff and leads) adds mapped members. |
| `GET /api/admin/teams` | `teams:manage` | Paginated list with `ownerType`, `ownerId`, `kind`, `q` (name in every language) and sort. |
| `POST /api/admin/teams` | `teams:manage` | Create a team: `ownerType`, `ownerId`, `kind`, `name`, `description`, `parentId`, `capacity`, `sort`. |
| `GET /api/admin/teams/:id` | `teams:manage` | One team, with `ownerLabel` and `memberCount`. |
| `PATCH /api/admin/teams/:id` | `teams:manage` | Edit `name`, `description`, `parentId`, `capacity`, `sort`. |
| `DELETE /api/admin/teams/:id` | `teams:manage` | Delete a team; its members and its own rules go with it (foreign key cascades). |
| `POST /api/admin/teams/:id/members` | `teams:manage` | Add `{ userId, role, status? }`. `400` for a role off the ladder, `409` for a member already. |
| `PATCH /api/admin/teams/:id/members/:userId` | `teams:manage` | Change `role` and `status`. |
| `DELETE /api/admin/teams/:id/members/:userId` | `teams:manage` | Remove an explicit member. |
| `GET /api/admin/teams/mappings` | `teams:manage` | Rules, by `ownerType` and `ownerId`, or those covering `teamId`. |
| `POST /api/admin/teams/mappings` | `teams:manage` | Add a rule. |
| `PATCH /api/admin/teams/mappings/:id` | `teams:manage` | Edit `teamId`, `match`, `teamRole`, `priority`. |
| `DELETE /api/admin/teams/mappings/:id` | `teams:manage` | Delete a rule. |
| `GET /api/admin/teams/ladders` | `teams:manage` | Every ladder, with `source: 'stored' \| 'default'`. |
| `GET /api/admin/teams/ladders/:kind` | `teams:manage` | One ladder. |
| `PUT /api/admin/teams/ladders/:kind` | `teams:manage` | Replace `{ roles, leadFrom }`. `409 team_role_in_use`. |
| `GET /api/admin/teams/users` | `teams:manage` | `?q=` finds people to add by name; with `teams:pii` also by email, and the answer has the emails. |

Adding and removing members is staff only. Leads hold `teams:lead`, which other modules check, but they do not manage
their team's members in this release.

`status` on a membership is set by the module that uses it, such as crew; see [Membership status](#membership-status).

## Database

Tables the module adds. Generate and commit a [migration](/get-started/database#migrations) after installing.

| Table | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `teams` | `tem_` id, `ownerType`, `ownerId` (null for the platform), `parentId`, `kind`, Translated `name` and `description`, `capacity`, `sort`. |
| `team_members` | `tmm_` id, `teamId` (cascade), `userId`, `role`, `status`, `addedBy`. One row per team and user. |
| `team_role_ladders` | `kind`, ordered `roles` (`[{ key, label }]`) and `leadFrom`. |
| `team_role_mappings` | `trm_` id, owner, `teamId` (cascade, null for every team), `match`, `teamRole`, `priority`. |

User ids have no foreign key: the users table belongs to Users. Whoever deletes a user removes their memberships.

## Translations

Override any string in your app's `i18n/locales/<code>.json`. The module registers `da` and `en` files under
`teams.*`. See [overriding module strings](/modules/platform/module-kit/i18n#overriding-module-strings-in-your-app).

Key groups: `menu.*`, `permissions.*`, `scopes.*` and `admin.*` (with `admin.tabs.*`, `admin.ownerTypes.*` and
`admin.sources.*`). Ladder role labels are data: edit them on the ladder.

## Testing notes

The e2e fixture runs on sqlite without Authorization, Subscriptions or OAuth Server. Roles come from a role provider,
plan rules from a stand-in `teams:roles:resolve` listener, and mapped member candidates from a
`teams:members:candidates` listener. The claim is covered by a unit test of the plugin. The postgresql branch of the
list's name search has no end-to-end test.

## Not in this release

- **"My teams" dashboard card**, with your role and the lead's contact. It waits for the dashboard module and lands in
  a follow-up through `extendDashboard`, behind `hasNuxtModule('@frigear-nu/dashboard')`.
- **"My teams" account page.** `GET /api/teams/me` serves it already.
- **Website wiring and crew presets.** Crew adds its presets as `team_role_mappings` rows.
- **Materialised mapped members.** A mapped member could become a row on their first action, such as taking a shift,
  so history stays stable when a rule changes. Today mapped members are never stored.
- **Leads managing members.** `teams:lead` routes to add and remove members of your own team.

## Open questions

- Should crew materialise a mapped member when they take a shift, or should shifts store the role they held?
- Should an owner's visibility (a public operation) let anyone read its teams, as the plan suggested? Today only
  members and staff do.
- Should `teams:members:candidates` move into Auth as a way to enumerate the holders of a role? That would be a core
  change, so Teams owns the hook for now.
