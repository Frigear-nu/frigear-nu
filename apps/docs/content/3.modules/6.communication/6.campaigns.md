---
title: Campaigns
description: Lists people sign up to, also without an account, and campaigns to their subscribers and to users by email, SMS and push, with link and open tracking and one-click unsubscribe.
---

Campaigns gives your app lists that people sign up to with an email address, a phone number or both: the readers of your news,
the volunteers of an event, the members of a club. They do not need an account. A new subscriber confirms from a link in an email or SMS (double opt-in),
and the subscription stores the proof of their consent. Every message carries a signed link to unsubscribe again.
Signed-in users manage their subscriptions on their account page, and staff manage the lists in the admin area.

Staff send **campaigns**: to the subscribers of one or more lists (email and SMS), or to users picked by role, team,
operation or by name (email, SMS and push). Campaigns go out in batches from a scheduled
task, now or at a set time. Links in them are counted per recipient through
[Short Links](/modules/communication/short-links), opens optionally, and every email carries one-click unsubscribe
headers.

The package is `@frigear-nu/campaigns`. Every message goes through [Notify](/modules/communication/notify), on the
email, SMS and push drivers your app sets. Campaigns ships no SMS provider of its own.

## What you get

- **Signup:** the global `<ListSignup list="…">` component, also usable as `::list-signup{list="…"}` in
  Nuxt Content, and a plain HTML form for other sites, shown on each list's staff page.
- **Public pages:**
  - `/subscriptions/confirm`, where the link in the confirmation message leads. It confirms on a button, never when the
    page opens, so link scanners and mail previews confirm nothing. After a plain HTML form signup it says "check your
    inbox".
  - `/subscriptions/unsubscribe`, where the unsubscribe link leads. It also acts on a button only.
- **Account page:** `/account/subscriptions`, the signed-in user's subscriptions, with an item in the account menu when
  [Menu](/modules/interface/menu) is installed.
- **Admin area** (needs [Admin](/modules/interface/admin)):
  - `/admin/campaigns`, the campaigns, and `/admin/campaigns/:id`, one campaign. See [Staff screens](#staff-screens).
  - `/admin/campaigns/lists`, the lists with their subscriber counts.
  - `/admin/campaigns/lists/:id`, one list: its settings, its subscribers and the signup code to copy.
  - A "Campaigns" item in the Manage group of the admin sidebar, when Menu is installed. Tabs under the heading switch
    between campaigns and lists.
- **Email templates** `campaigns-confirm` and `campaigns-message`, when
  [Email Templates](/modules/communication/email-templates) is installed.
- **Notification topic** `campaigns.message` on the account's notifications page (with
  [Preferences](/modules/identity/preferences)): email and push on, SMS off until the user turns it on.
- **Tasks** `campaigns:deliver` (every minute) and `campaigns:retention` (nightly), also on Cloudflare cron triggers.
- **Campaign API** for staff: drafts, a preview of who they reach, send, schedule, cancel, stats and deliveries.

:module-runtime{server client}

::note
**Installed for you:** Auth, Users, Notify, Short Links, UI and Module Kit.

## Quick setup

::steps

### Install Campaigns

```bash
pnpm add @frigear-nu/campaigns
```

### Add it to `nuxt.config.ts`

```ts [nuxt.config.ts]
export default defineNuxtConfig({
  modules: ['@nuxthub/core', '@frigear-nu/users', '@frigear-nu/admin', '@frigear-nu/campaigns'],
  hub: { db: 'sqlite', kv: true },
})
```

Unsubscribe links are signed with a key derived from Nuxt's `appSecret`. Set it in production, as your sessions need
it too:

```bash [.env]
NUXT_APP_SECRET=a-long-random-string-of-at-least-32-characters
```

### Generate and commit a migration

Campaigns adds seven tables (and Short Links two). Generate a [migration](/modules/platform/module-kit/database) and commit it.

```bash
npx nuxt db generate
```

### Create a list and put its signup on a page

Open `/admin/campaigns/lists` as an admin and create a list. Then add its signup to a page:

```vue [app/pages/news.vue]
<template>
  <ListSignup list="news" />
</template>
```

::

::callout{color="success" icon="i-lucide-check"}
**What you should see:** a signup sends a confirmation email. Its link opens `/subscriptions/confirm`, and after the
click the subscriber shows up as subscribed on the list's staff page.
::

## Options

Options go under the `campaigns` key.

| Option | Type | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `rateLimit` | `number` | `10` | Signups per IP address and list in the window. `0` turns the limit off. |
| `rateLimitWindowSeconds` | `number` | `600` | The window of the rate limit. |
| `confirmTtlHours` | `number` | `72` | How long a confirmation link works. |
| `resendAfterSeconds` | `number` | `300` | A new signup for an address that is still waiting to confirm sends the confirmation again only after this pause. |
| `corsOrigins` | `string[]` | `[]` | Other sites allowed to post to the subscribe route from the browser. A plain HTML form needs none. Env `NUXT_CAMPAIGNS_CORS_ORIGINS` (a JSON array). |
| `captcha` | `false \| 'turnstile' \| 'recaptcha' \| 'hcaptcha'` | `false` | A captcha the subscribe route checks. It needs [Forms](/modules/features/forms), whose site and secret keys it uses. |
| `signingKey` | `string` | from `appSecret` | The key unsubscribe links, delivery tokens and IP hashes are signed with. Env `NUXT_CAMPAIGNS_SIGNING_KEY`. Without it and without `appSecret`, signups answer 501 `not_configured`. |
| `batchSize` | `number` | `200` | Messages the deliver task sends per run. Env `NUXT_CAMPAIGNS_BATCH_SIZE`. |
| `deliverCron` | `string \| false` | `* * * * *` | When `campaigns:deliver` runs. `false` registers the task without a schedule (run it yourself). |
| `retentionDays` | `number \| false` | `180` | Days the per-recipient rows of a finished campaign are kept; its counts stay. `false` keeps them and schedules no retention task. Env `NUXT_CAMPAIGNS_RETENTION_DAYS`. |
| `settings.pages.confirm` | `string \| boolean` | `/subscriptions/confirm` | The confirm page. If you turn it off, serve your own page at the same path: confirmation links point there. |
| `settings.pages.unsubscribe` | `string \| boolean` | `/subscriptions/unsubscribe` | The unsubscribe page. |
| `settings.pages.account` | `string \| boolean` | `/account/subscriptions` | The signed-in user's subscriptions. |
| `settings.pages.adminLists` | `string \| boolean` | `/admin/campaigns/lists` | The staff page of the lists (with Admin). |
| `settings.pages.adminList` | `string \| boolean` | `/admin/campaigns/lists/:id` | One list's staff page. It must contain `:id`. |
| `settings.pages.adminCampaigns` | `string \| boolean` | `/admin/campaigns` | The staff list of campaigns (with Admin). |
| `settings.pages.adminCampaign` | `string \| boolean` | `/admin/campaigns/:id` | One campaign's staff page. It must contain `:id`. |

## Permissions

Campaigns registers these permissions with [Authorization](/modules/identity/authorization).

| Name | Who holds it | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `campaigns:manage` | `admin` | Lists and their subscribers without addresses; campaign drafts, previews and stats. |
| `campaigns:send` | `admin` | Sending, scheduling and cancelling campaigns. |
| `campaigns:send-important` | `admin` | Creating and sending an important message, which reaches users on channels they turned off. |
| `campaigns:pii` | `admin` (sensitive) | Subscribers' email addresses and phone numbers, searching by them, and a campaign's deliveries. Never covered by `campaigns:*`. |
| `campaigns:subscriptions` | Every signed-in user (implicit) | The user's own subscriptions. Also a token scope, through the `subscriptions` scope. |

The signup, confirm, unsubscribe and open-pixel routes are anonymous. The rate limit, the optional captcha and the tokens in the
messages guard them.

## How signing up works

- **One answer for every address.** The subscribe route answers `{ confirm }` whether the address was known or not,
  subscribed or not, so it never tells anyone who is on a list. A subscriber who signs up again gets no email.
- **Contacts.** A contact is one email address and/or one phone number, unique each. An anonymous signup never changes
  an existing contact. When a signed-in user's email address is verified, the contact with that address is linked to
  them.
- **Double opt-in.** With the list's "Ask new subscribers to confirm" on (the default), a new subscription is
  `pending` until the link in the confirmation email or SMS is followed and confirmed. Without it, it is `active` at
  once. A signed-in user who turns on a list from the account page with a verified address is active at once.
- **Consent proof.** Each subscription stores where the consent came from (`form`, `api`, `admin`, `forms` or
  `account`), the version of the consent text the person agreed to, when they asked, when they confirmed and a keyed
  hash of their IP address. Changing a list's consent text starts a new version.
- **Unsubscribe links** are `<subscription id>.<signature>`, an HMAC-SHA-256 under the signing key. Nothing is stored
  per link, so every message to a subscriber can carry one. Unsubscribing clears a confirmation that is still waiting.
- **The account page** turns the email channel on or off. An SMS subscription can be turned off there; signing up for
  SMS goes through the signup, which confirms the phone number.
- **A honeypot.** The signup sends a hidden `website` field. When it is filled in, the route answers as usual and
  stores nothing.

## Campaigns

A campaign goes **to lists** (`kind: 'newsletter'`) or **to people** (`kind: 'message'`). The stored kinds keep
their first names; the screens say "To lists" and "To people". Staff write it as a draft,
check who it reaches with the preview, then send it now or schedule it.

| | To lists (`newsletter`) | To people (`message`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience | `listIds`: the active subscribers of these lists, each contact once per channel | `grants` and/or `userIds`: the members of each grant (role, team, operation) and these users, each once |
| Channels | `email`, `sms` | `email`, `sms`, `push` |
| Who decides | The subscription: only `active` ones get it, checked again right before each send | The user's choices for `campaigns.message`; an `important` campaign ignores them |
| Unsubscribe | From every list of the campaign, on that channel | Turns the channel off for `campaigns.message` |

- **Content** is per channel and per language: `email: { subject, body }`, `sms: { body }`, `push: { title, body, url }`.
  The email body is markdown (bold, italics, links, paragraphs), rendered and escaped on the server. Each recipient gets
  their own language (the contact's or user's), then the campaign's `locale`, then English.
- **The audience of a campaign to people** is resolved when it is sent or scheduled, since grant kinds need the request. `public` and
  `signed-in` grants are refused, and an audience that names nobody is 400 `audience_not_resolved`. The lists of a
  campaign to lists are read when it goes out; sending one now to lists nobody is on is 400 `audience_not_resolved` too.
- **Delivery.** Sending makes the campaign `scheduled`; the `campaigns:deliver` task starts it (`sending`), adds one
  delivery row per recipient and channel in pages (resumable), and sends `batchSize` of them per run. A run claims rows
  with an update guarded by their status, so two runs never send the same row. A claim older than ten minutes is tried
  again, up to three times. "Send now" also starts one run right after the answer. When every row is done the campaign
  is `sent` and its counts are kept on it. Addresses are never copied into the rows: they are read when each message
  goes out.
- **Links** with `trackClicks` (on by default): each distinct `http(s)` link becomes a short link owned by the campaign,
  and each recipient's message points at `/l/<code>/<delivery token>`. A click redirects to the link and counts on the
  delivery (`clickedAt`, `clicks`) and per link. Anchors, paths, `mailto:` and `tel:` links stay as written.
- **Opens** with `trackOpens` (off by default): the email carries a 1x1 image from `/api/campaigns/o/<token>`; the first
  load marks the delivery opened. Mail apps that load images on their own count as an open too.
- **Unsubscribe.** The delivery token is the delivery id signed like a subscription token. Every email has
  `List-Unsubscribe: <…/api/campaigns/unsubscribe/<token>>` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, so
  mail apps show their own unsubscribe button, and a footer with the unsubscribe page and the subscriptions (or
  notification settings) page. An SMS ends with the unsubscribe page.
- **Skipped** rows say why: `disabled` (the user turned the channel off), `unavailable` (no address, phone or device),
  `unsubscribed` (the subscriber left before their turn) or `cancelled`.
- **Retention.** `campaigns:retention` deletes the delivery and click rows of campaigns finished more than
  `retentionDays` ago; the counts per channel stay on the campaign. Clicks per link are counted from those rows, so
  they go with them.

## Staff screens

The campaign pages need [Admin](/modules/interface/admin). They sit under one "Campaigns" item of the admin sidebar,
with tabs to the lists.

- **`/admin/campaigns`** lists the campaigns, newest first, with their status, kind, channels, audience, when they
  went (or go) out and how many were sent, opened and clicked. Past eight campaigns a toolbar searches by name and
  filters by status and kind. Each row has a menu: Open, Edit campaign, Duplicate, Cancel campaign and Delete campaign,
  as far as the campaign's status and your permissions allow.
- **Create campaign** opens a form: the kind (to lists or to people, fixed once created), the channels, the audience
  (lists to send to, or people and groups such as roles, teams and operations), the texts per channel and
  language, and whether to count clicks (on by default) and opens (off by default). A draft may be unfinished; the
  texts of one channel go together.
- **`/admin/campaigns/:id`** shows one campaign: what keeps a draft from being sent, a summary, the audience with how
  many it reaches now per channel, the content per channel and language (the email rendered as recipients read it,
  without its footer), and once it goes out the results: counts, skipped rows by reason, clicks per link and, with
  `campaigns:pii`, the recipients.
- **Send campaign** first counts who it reaches and asks you to confirm with those numbers. **Schedule campaign** asks
  for a date and time in your own time zone. A scheduled or sending campaign can be cancelled.

| Action | Needs | Only when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See campaigns, their audience, content and results | `campaigns:manage` | |
| Create, edit, duplicate and delete | `campaigns:manage` | Edit and delete: a draft |
| Send now, schedule | `campaigns:send` | A draft with nothing missing |
| Cancel | `campaigns:send` | Scheduled or sending |
| Make a campaign to people important, or edit, send or duplicate an important one | `campaigns:send-important` as well | |
| See the recipients of each delivery | `campaigns:pii` | Once it went out |

The pages hide what you may not do; the routes check again.

## The signup component

```vue
<ListSignup list="news" />
<ListSignup list="news" title="Get our news" description="Once a month, no more." />
<ListSignup list="news" :title="false" />
```

| Prop | Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `list` | `string` | The list's URL name. Required. |
| `title` | `string \| false` | Replaces the list's name as the heading; `false` shows none. |
| `description` | `string` | Replaces the list's description. |

It asks for a name, an email address and a phone number (the channels the list has), shows the consent text next
to the checkbox and posts in the visitor's locale, which the confirmation message uses. With the `captcha` option it
shows the captcha widget of Forms.

In a Nuxt Content page:

```md
::list-signup{list="news"}
::
```

**Another site.** Each list's staff page has a plain HTML `<form>` to paste into a site that cannot run the
component. It posts to the subscribe route, which redirects the visitor to `/subscriptions/confirm` to check their inbox.
A site that posts with JavaScript instead needs its origin in `corsOrigins`.

## Routes

Path builders come from `@frigear-nu/campaigns/api` (`campaignsApi`).

| Route | Who | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/campaigns/lists/:slug` | Anyone | A public list as the signup shows it. A list that is not public is a 404. |
| `POST /api/campaigns/lists/:slug/subscribe` | Anyone (rate limited) | `{ email?, phone?, name?, locale?, channels?, consent, captcha? }`, as JSON or a plain form. Answers `{ confirm }`; a plain form post gets a 303 to the confirm page. 429 `rate_limited`, 400 `captcha_required` / `captcha_failed`. |
| `POST /api/campaigns/confirm/:token` | The token | Confirms the subscription. 400 `invalid_token` (unknown or used), 410 `token_expired`. |
| `POST /api/campaigns/unsubscribe/:token` | The token | Unsubscribes what the token names: a subscription, or a campaign message's lists (answers like a subscription) or `campaigns.message` channel (answers `{ topic, channel, status }`). The RFC 8058 one-click target: the body is not read. 400 `invalid_token`. |
| `GET /api/campaigns/o/:token` | The token | The 1x1 GIF of a campaign email; marks it opened with `trackOpens`. The same image for any token. |
| `GET`, `PATCH /api/campaigns/me/subscriptions` | `campaigns:subscriptions` | The user's subscriptions; `PATCH { lists: { [listId]: { email?, sms? } } }` turns channels on (email only) or off. |
| `GET`, `POST /api/admin/campaigns/lists` | `campaigns:manage` | Every list with its counts; creates one. 409 `conflict` for a URL name in use. |
| `GET`, `PATCH`, `DELETE /api/admin/campaigns/lists/:id` | `campaigns:manage` | One list. Deleting it deletes its subscriptions. |
| `GET /api/admin/campaigns/lists/:id/subscribers` | `campaigns:manage` | Paginated subscriptions, `?status&channel&q&sort&order`. Addresses with `campaigns:pii`. |
| `GET`, `POST /api/admin/campaigns` | `campaigns:manage` | Paginated campaigns, `?status&kind&q&sort&order`; creates a draft. An `important` one needs `campaigns:send-important`. |
| `GET`, `PATCH`, `DELETE /api/admin/campaigns/:id` | `campaigns:manage` | One campaign. Only a draft changes or is deleted: 409 `not_draft`. |
| `GET /api/admin/campaigns/:id/preview` | `campaigns:manage` | `{ recipients, channels }`: who it reaches now, per channel. For a campaign to people, the most it can reach. |
| `POST /api/admin/campaigns/:id/send` | `campaigns:send` | Sends a draft now. 400 `validation_failed` (not ready) or `audience_not_resolved`, 409 `not_draft`. |
| `POST /api/admin/campaigns/:id/schedule` | `campaigns:send` | `{ scheduledAt }` in the future. |
| `POST /api/admin/campaigns/:id/cancel` | `campaigns:send` | Stops a scheduled or sending campaign; unsent rows are skipped. 409 `not_cancellable`. |
| `GET /api/admin/campaigns/:id/stats` | `campaigns:manage` | Counts per channel (`total`, `queued`, `sent`, `skipped`, `failed`, `opened`, `clicked`, `clicks`), `skipReasons` (skipped rows by reason, while the rows are kept) and clicks per link. |
| `GET /api/admin/campaigns/:id/deliveries` | `campaigns:manage` and `campaigns:pii` | Paginated deliveries with their recipient, `?status&channel&sort&order`. |
| `GET /api/admin/campaigns/users` | `campaigns:manage` | People for the form of a campaign to people: `?q=` searches names (at most 20), `?ids=1,2` names the people a draft lists. With `campaigns:pii` the search matches emails too and the answer carries them. |

Sending and scheduling an important message needs `campaigns:send-important` as well.

## Database

Tables the module adds. Generate and commit a [migration](/modules/platform/module-kit/database) after installing.

| Table | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `campaign_lists` | `id` (`cml_`), `slug` (unique), `name`, `description`, `channels`, `doubleOptIn`, `public`, `consentText`, `consentVersion`, `formId` and `formFields` (a forms form that signs people up). |
| `campaign_contacts` | `id` (`cmc_`), `email` (lower-cased, unique), `phone` (E.164, unique), `userId`, `name`, `locale`. |
| `campaign_subscriptions` | `id` (`cms_`), `listId`, `contactId`, `channel` (unique together), `status` (`pending`, `active`, `unsubscribed`), `confirmTokenHash` (SHA-256 of the confirmation token), `confirmExpiresAt`, `source`, `consentVersion`, `requestedAt`, `confirmedAt`, `ipHash`, `unsubscribedAt`. |
| `campaigns` | `id` (`cmp_`), `kind`, `name`, `channels`, `audience`, `content`, `locale`, `trackClicks`, `trackOpens`, `important`, `status` (`draft`, `scheduled`, `sending`, `sent`, `cancelled`), `scheduledAt`, `startedAt`, `materializedAt` and `materializeCursor` (delivery rows added so far), `finishedAt`, `cancelledAt`, `recipientUserIds` (a message's resolved audience), `origin` (the site links point at), `createdBy`, `sentBy`, `counts`. |
| `campaign_links` | `id` (`cmk_`), `campaignId`, `shortLinkId` (a `short_links` row owned by `campaigns` and the campaign), `url`, `position`. |
| `campaign_deliveries` | `id` (`cmd_`), `campaignId`, `channel`, `userId` or `contactId` (+ `subscriptionId`), `status` (`queued`, `sending`, `sent`, `skipped`, `failed`), `skipReason`, `attempts`, `claimId`, `claimedAt`, `sentAt`, `openedAt`, `clickedAt`, `clicks`, `error`. No address. |
| `campaign_clicks` | `deliveryId`, `linkId`, `at`: one row per counted click, for clicks per link. |

## Translations

Override any string in your app's `i18n/locales/<code>.json`, under the `campaigns` key. Campaigns ships Danish and
English, and so do the confirmation email and SMS and the footers of campaign messages.
