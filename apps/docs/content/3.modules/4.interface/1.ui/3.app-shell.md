`AppShell` is the chrome of the admin area. The `admin` layout is a thin wrapper that feeds it its menus; an area of
your own does the same:

```vue
<!-- layouts/admin.vue -->
<AppShell
  id="admin"
  :title="t('title', 'Administration')"
  home="/admin"
  :navigation="navigation"
  :nav-label="t('nav.label', 'Admin navigation')"
  :search-groups="searchGroups"
  :back-to="{ to: '/', label: t('backToSite', 'Back to the site') }"
>
  <template #sidebar-footer="{ collapsed }">
    <AccountMenu block :collapsed="collapsed" />
  </template>
  <slot />
</AppShell>
```

- `id` keys the `dashboard-sidebar-<id>` cookie (collapse and width), so give each area its own. `home` and `backTo.to` are
  raw paths: the shell adds the locale prefix.
- `navigation` is a list of `UNavigationMenu` groups; `type: 'label'` rows head a group. The navbar shows a muted
  eyebrow, `title › active section`, never a heading: the page renders the only `<h1>` through `PageHeading`. The
  document title defaults to the active section (else `title`); a page's own `useHead` wins.
- `searchGroups` adds the ⌘K button and palette (colour mode is not part of it, it lives in the account menu). Leave it
  out for no search.
- The body is centred at `max-w-7xl`. `wide` (or `definePageMeta({ wide: true })` on a page) makes it full width.
- On small screens the navbar opens the sidebar as a drawer that closes on navigation. The navbar's right side shows
  `LanguageSelect` when the app has one and more than one locale, unless `navbar-right` is filled.
- The light app theme is utility classes on the shell root (`bg-default` body, `bg-elevated/25` sidebar, a
  primary-coloured brand tile); a host can restyle the areas through the `[data-area=app]` attribute.
- The shell is the page's scroll container (the panel scrolls, not the document), so hash links behave as they do in
  the admin area.
